About Our School

Government Higher Secondary School, Kuruvadi was established to impart standard education to rural students with a strong moral foundation. Our experienced staff members provide comprehensive guidance that enables every student to discover their hidden talents and potential. With well-equipped classrooms and modern learning methodologies, our institution continuously strives for 100% academic results and holistic student success.

About Our Computer Lab

Even in government schools, advanced computer education and modern digital facilities are provided to bridge the digital gap for poor and underprivileged students. Through practical hands-on computer training, our students gain technological confidence, develop problem-solving skills, and successfully step forward to achieve bright careers in life.
