About Our School

Government Higher Secondary School, Kuruvadi was founded to offer high-quality education and equal learning opportunities for every student in our rural community. With dedicated teachers and supportive infrastructure, we focus on both academic excellence and holistic personality development. Our school empowers underprivileged students to acquire modern skills and achieve great success in higher studies and future careers.
